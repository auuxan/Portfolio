About

Not a developer.
But I ship.

I'm an in-house legal officer at Ocean Life Insurance — which means I spend my days navigating OIC regulations, reviewing contracts, and coordinating with government agencies. That's the job description.

But I noticed early on that a significant portion of legal work is repetitive and process-driven. So I started building systems to handle it — first small scripts, then full automation pipelines using Google Apps Script and the Gemini API.

None of these started as official projects. They started as a personal frustration with inefficiency and a willingness to figure things out. Four of them are now running in production.
